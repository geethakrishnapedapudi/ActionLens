\# ActionLens



\### Turn complex documents into personalized decisions and clear next actions.



ActionLens is a document intelligence and decision-support platform that transforms unstructured requirement-based documents into structured, personalized guidance.



Instead of simply summarizing a document, ActionLens answers a more useful question:



> \*\*“What does this document mean for me, and what should I do next?”\*\*



\---



\## 🚀 The Problem



Important opportunities and requirements are often buried inside long documents such as:



\- Scholarship notifications

\- Job notifications

\- Internship announcements

\- Government schemes

\- Grants and funding opportunities

\- Competitions and challenges

\- Admission notices

\- Fellowships

\- Rules and policy documents



Users may struggle to identify eligibility conditions, deadlines, required documents, benefits, and the correct next steps.



Traditional document summarizers provide information.



\*\*ActionLens turns that information into a personalized action plan.\*\*
