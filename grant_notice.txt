STARTUP INNOVATION GRANT 2026

ELIGIBILITY

Applicants must:
1. Be a registered startup in India.
2. Have been operating for at least 1 year.
3. Have annual revenue below INR 5,00,00,000.
4. Have a technology-based innovation project.

BENEFIT

Selected startups may receive funding of up to INR 10,00,000.

DEADLINE

Applications close on 25 October 2026.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

1. Startup registration certificate
2. Project proposal
3. Financial statement
4. Company bank details

APPLICATION PROCESS

1. Complete the online application.
2. Upload required documents.
3. Submit before the deadline.

IMPORTANT

Incomplete applications may be rejected.
