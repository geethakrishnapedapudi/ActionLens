DIGITAL BUSINESS SUPPORT SCHEME 2026

ELIGIBILITY

Applicants must:
1. Be an Indian citizen.
2. Own a registered small business.
3. Have annual business revenue below INR 50,00,000.
4. Operate the business within India.

BENEFITS

Eligible applicants may receive financial assistance and digital training.

DEADLINE

Applications close on 10 November 2026.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

1. Government identity proof
2. Business registration certificate
3. Bank account details
4. Revenue statement

APPLICATION PROCESS

1. Register online.
2. Provide business information.
3. Upload supporting documents.
4. Submit the application.

IMPORTANT

All information provided must be accurate.
