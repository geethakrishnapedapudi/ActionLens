NATIONAL STUDENT TECHNOLOGY SCHOLARSHIP 2026

Applications are invited from undergraduate students pursuing technology,
engineering, computer science, and related programs.

ELIGIBILITY
Applicants must:
1. Be currently enrolled in a recognized undergraduate institution in India.
2. Have a minimum academic score of 70%.
3. Have annual family income below INR 6,00,000.
4. Be an Indian citizen.

BENEFITS
Selected students will receive:
- Financial assistance of INR 50,000.
- Access to a six-month technology mentorship program.
- Certificate of participation.

IMPORTANT DATE
Application deadline: 30 September 2026.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
Applicants must submit:
1. College identity card.
2. Academic mark sheet.
3. Income certificate.
4. Proof of citizenship.
5. Bank account details.

APPLICATION PROCESS
1. Complete the online application form.
2. Upload all required documents.
3. Verify the submitted information.
4. Submit the application before the deadline.

IMPORTANT
Incomplete applications may not be considered.
Applicants are responsible for ensuring that all information and documents
are accurate before submission.
