NATIONAL INNOVATION CHALLENGE 2026

ELIGIBILITY

Participants must:
1. Be between 18 and 35 years of age.
2. Be an Indian citizen.
3. Submit an original technology-based solution.
4. Submit the project before the deadline.

BENEFITS

Finalists will receive certificates and mentorship opportunities.

DEADLINE

Project submissions close on 20 October 2026.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

1. Project description
2. Participant identity proof
3. Source code repository link

APPLICATION PROCESS

1. Register for the challenge.
2. Develop the solution.
3. Submit the project and required information.
4. Attend the final presentation if selected.

IMPORTANT

Plagiarized or previously submitted projects may be disqualified.
